New `Meter` for how much of a known total is used (a budget, a goal), which grows from empty when it appears, and motion helpers for app code on the motion tokens: `rupa-enter` for a new row or card, `rupa-pop` for a status that just changed, `rupa-grow-x` for a bar that fills. All of them finish at once when the user prefers reduced motion.
